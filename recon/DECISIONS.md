# Product decisions

The owner signed up for and used Higgsfield, and took these screenshots on **19 Oct 2026**; the onboarding and signed-in Image page screenshots support that owner-reported recon. Codex inspected these supplied images, rather than performing the owner's signup or product session.

## Screenshot evidence and decisions

1. [01-ai-influencer.png](01-ai-influencer.png): AI Influencer shows Login/Sign up, a Character type count of 9, Create Influencer/Motion controls, optional photo upload and Explore/History tabs; Slate cut the influencer workflow to focus on prompt-to-take iteration without character tooling.
2. [02-onboarding-goals.png](02-onboarding-goals.png): Onboarding asks "What do you want to achieve with Higgsfield?" and presents six goal cards beside a large scene image; Slate cut signup and goal onboarding so the mock workspace opens directly.
3. [03-image-composer.png](03-image-composer.png): The Image page shows an account avatar, Assets, a scene prompt, GPT Image 2, Auto aspect, High quality, 2K resolution, a 1/4 count control and Generate priced at 6.5 with 8.5 struck through; Slate kept cost on Generate but changed it to an exact rate-times-duration-times-resolution formula without promotional prices.
4. [04-model-picker.png](04-model-picker.png): The open picker shows a search box and ten visible model entries with Premium and New tags on some entries; Slate changed the model catalogue to three mock models with plain per-second rates to make cost comparison quick.
5. [05-mcp-discovery.png](05-mcp-discovery.png): The MCP page shows a search/category sidebar, Recreate aura edits and Product review UGC cards with Try in ChatGPT buttons, plus part of an Ad Recreator card; Slate cut MCP discovery and integrations to prioritize its local creation and review loop.
6. [06-mcp-faceless.png](06-mcp-faceless.png): The MCP page has Faceless selected and shows Editorial Motion Graphics, Stickman cartoon and part of Paper Diorama, with example imagery and Try in ChatGPT buttons on the first two cards; Slate cut this template/integration catalogue because it is outside the single mock video workflow.

The screenshots show a dense top navigation of about 15 product destinations, alongside pricing/discount badges (30% or 50% OFF in the captured views); Slate changed this to two destinations, Create and My takes, to keep creation and review easy to find.

Slate also uses a restrained charcoal/sage palette and a composer above a personal take library, rather than the reference's promotional discovery surfaces. Cost and Mock render status are explicit; active jobs reserve credits and failed/cancelled attempts release them.

## Image handling

All six supplied PNGs were visually checked for personal data before committing: no email addresses, account names, API keys or tokens were visible; small corner avatars were retained as authorized. The screenshots were renamed and moved without image editing, and SHA256 hashes matched before and after each move. This is a visual inspection, not a claim about hidden metadata.
